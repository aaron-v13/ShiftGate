# ShiftGate: Cover Image Design Brief

## 1. Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1280 x 720 px (16:9 aspect ratio standard for Lablab.ai cover card).
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG (< 5MB).
- **Intended Audience**: Hackathon judges, manufacturing engineers, AI practitioners.

---

## 2. Visual Theme & Aesthetic
- **Atmosphere**: Modern, high-tech industrial aesthetic combining clean manufacturing hardware cues with modern software telemetry.
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Primary Background**: Deep industrial slate / navy charcoal (`#0B0F19` to `#111827`).
  - **AMD Accent**: Vibrant AMD Radeon Red / Crimson (`#ED1C24` or `#E02424`) as primary accent glow and badge highlight.
  - **Gate Status Accents**:
    - Safety Block Alert: Crimson Red (`#EF4444`)
    - Verified / Allowed Status: Emerald Green (`#10B981`)
    - Warning / Shift Detection: Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`)
  - **Typography & Text**: High-contrast white (`#FFFFFF`) and slate muted grey (`#94A3B8`).

---

## 3. Layout Composition

### Left Half (60% Width): Typography & Value Proposition
- **Top Badge**:
  - Pill badge: `AMD DEVELOPER HACKATHON ACT III` with small AMD logo icon.
- **Main Headline (Bold, 54pt Sans-Serif)**:
  - **`ShiftGate`**
- **Sub-headline (24pt Sans-Serif)**:
  - **`Safe Reference Updates for Visual AI Inspection`**
- **Key Callout Pills**:
  - `AMD ROCm Execution` | `Zero Defect Escapes Policy` | `Station Audit Gate`
- **Bottom Footnote**:
  - `Track 1: Intelligent Industry`

### Right Half (40% Width): Visual Concept & Hero UI Teaser
- **Isometric / Perspective Split Device Card**:
  - A stylized inspection cockpit floating in subtle perspective with glowing edge highlights.
  - **Visual Elements**:
    1. A printed circuit board (PCB) assembly with highlighted component inspection bounding boxes.
    2. Split comparison badge: `v1_current` vs `v2_candidate_loose` showing a red **`🛑 BLOCK`** indicator over a flagged defect map.
    3. An overlay waveform / anomaly heatmap indicating PatchCore score localization.
    4. Small AMD ROCm badge in the corner of the device card (`Powered by AMD ROCm`).

---

## 4. Typography
- **Header Font**: Inter, Space Grotesk, or Montserrat (Bold / ExtraBold).
- **Body / Technical Labels**: JetBrains Mono or Roboto Mono (Medium).
- **Legibility**: All text must remain sharp and readable even when downscaled to mobile thumbnail view (320x180 px).

---
